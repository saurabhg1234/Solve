## Positioning and product structure

Mindmap positions itself as an enterprise AI product company installing sovereign AI inside client infrastructure, with forward deployed engineering and outcome-focused delivery. MindMap OS comprises the accelerator Library, Model Estate and Deploy Kit. The official OS page describes installed/versioned components with serving, routing, memory, observability, guardrails, connectors and audit. The stated library size is 117 accelerators and the stated customer count is 50+ enterprises; these are sourced company claims and should be checked for the particular released proposal version.

The supplied deck details AI Centres of Excellence, Service-as-a-Software, sovereign model families and industry solutions, plus engineering practices in test automation, RPA, data/analytics, digital engineering, work management, support, staffing and skilling. Recommendations must select the capabilities relevant to the client's evidence, not list everything Mindmap sells.

## Claims requiring reconciliation

The deck's broad production-family count should be reconciled with the precise statuses above before release. The homepage says it does not sell retainers, while the supplied deck includes a fixed-retainer billing model. The BRD separately defines its commercial structure and approval process. Preserve the source/version and have the pricing owner determine the correct engagement model for a pursuit rather than combining conflicting marketing statements into a commitment.

Customer stories and figures are source claims. Keep anonymised cases anonymised; retain denominators, time periods, measured/projected distinctions and the original evidence. A customer logo is not an endorsement. A published delivery range is not an agreed timeline for a new client; derive its plan from the approved design, reviewed effort and human gates.
